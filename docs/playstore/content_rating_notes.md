## Recommended Rating: **Teen (13+)**

### Justification:
- No violent, sexual, or otherwise objectionable content
- Contains advertising (interstitial ads may appear at intervals)
- No user interaction or social features
- No location or personal data collection
- All audio processing is local and private

---

## Additional Notes:
- The app includes a safety disclaimer for high-volume audio usage
- The Flame Lab feature includes experimental warnings and safety interlocks
- The app does not require or use microphone permissions
- All AI processing occurs locally on the device
