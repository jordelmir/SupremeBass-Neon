1. Introduction

Supreme Corp ("we," "our," or "us") operates the Supreme Acoustics mobile application (the "App"). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and protect information when you use our App. By using Supreme Acoustics, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

We are committed to protecting your privacy. This App is designed to work primarily on-device, and we minimize the data we collect and share.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Information Collected Automatically

  • Advertising ID: We use Google AdMob, which may collect your Advertising ID (AAID/GAID) for delivering personalized advertisements.
  • App Interaction Data: Anonymized data about how you interact with the App (features used, session duration, usage patterns).
  • Device Information: Device model, OS version, screen resolution, language settings.

2.2 Information We Do NOT Collect

Supreme Acoustics does NOT collect:

  • Audio data — All audio processing occurs locally on your device. No audio files, recordings, or sound data are transmitted.
  • Camera or visual data — Camera access is used only for on-device visual analysis. No images or video leave your device.
  • Contacts, Location, Personal files, Music library
  • Personally identifiable information

3. App Features and Data

Supreme Acoustics includes the following features, all of which process data locally:

  • Bass Booster: Real-time audio enhancement processed on-device. No audio data is transmitted.
  • Signal Generator: Generates test signals locally. No signal data leaves your device.
  • Device Lab: Characterizes device frequency response. All measurements stored locally.
  • Experiment Engine: Runs acoustic experiments on-device. Results stored locally.
  • Flame Lab: Experimental acoustic research with safety interlocks. All data local.
  • Visual Analysis: Uses camera for on-device brightness/contrast analysis. No visual data transmitted.

4. Third-Party Services

  • Google AdMob — Advertising and ad personalization. Privacy Policy
  • Google Play Services — Core app functionality. Privacy Policy

5. Artificial Intelligence

All AI processing is performed locally on your device. No audio data, sound profiles, or usage data is sent to external servers.

6. Data Retention

  • Advertising ID: Retained by Google AdMob per Google's policies.
  • Local data: All audio processing data, presets, measurements, and settings are stored locally and deleted when you uninstall.

7. Data Security

We implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect the information we collect. However, no method of transmission over the Internet is 100% secure.

8. Children's Privacy

Supreme Acoustics is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

9. Your Choices

  • Data Deletion: Clear App data through device settings or uninstall the App.
